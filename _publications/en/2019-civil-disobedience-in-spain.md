### Abstract

This study examines how the Catalan autonomy and civil disobedience processes in Spain were represented by international public broadcasters (BBC and TRT) through comparative discourse analysis. The research was presented at the 5th International Conference in Communication and Media Studies (*Think, Talk, Cultivate Peace*) and published as a book chapter in the proceedings book by EMU Press.

#### Keywords
Civil Disobedience, Media Representation, BBC, TRT, Catalan Autonomy, Political Communication, Peace Journalism.

With the evolution of communication technologies, transformations across the media landscape have become inevitable. Therefore, this course examines how digital journalism operates, how its technical infrastructure is built, and how news is delivered online. Furthermore, students learn how to build, maintain, and manage professional news portals through hands-on laboratory exercises.

#### Course Completion Requirements {#tamamlama-kosullari}

This course covers a concise history of the internet, key terminology, digital journalism principles, website deployment, news reporting, reader engagement via social channels, Google services integration, and journalism ethics. Each student is expected to manage both an individual portfolio website and collaborate on a shared group portal.

To successfully complete this course, students must satisfy the following criteria:

> ⚠️ **Attendance is mandatory for all students.** Attendance must be **at least 70%**. Students falling below this threshold will not receive a passing grade regardless of their practical scores.
{: .lesson-warning}

> ℹ️ **Weekly Laboratory Exercises:** Hands-on lab assignments must be completed and submitted on schedule every week. These exercises account for **50%** of your final grade.
{: .lesson-info}

> ℹ️ **Capstone Project:** You will build, launch, and manage a collaborative digital news portal project, including individual reporting articles and project documentation. This constitutes **50%** of your final grade.
{: .lesson-info}

#### Exams & Projects {#detayli-notlandirma}

Evaluation is based entirely on continuous practical assessments and the final capstone project.

- **Practicals:** 50%
- **Final Project:** 50%

#### Course Format

This course combines theoretical foundations with extensive practical laboratory sessions. Classes consist of instructor presentations followed by hands-on student lab work conducted at individual workstations.

#### Learning Outcomes {#ders-ciktilari}

Students who successfully complete this course will be able to:

- Understand the fundamental dynamics of digital journalism and modern web technologies.
- Manage domain names, web hosting environments, DNS records, and secure FTP deployments.
- Install, configure, secure, and administer the WordPress content management system.
- Implement search engine optimization (SEO) techniques and digital analytics tools.
- Collaborate effectively in teams to design, launch, and maintain a professional online news portal.

#### Course Policy & Important Notes {#onemli-notlar}

> ℹ️ **Technical Infrastructure Policy:** Students are responsible for procuring their own domain name and hosting plan required for setting up and administering news portals. Both individual testing sites and group production portals must be hosted on student-procured servers.
{: .lesson-info}

> ⚠️ **Content Ethics & Academic Integrity:** These portals serve as your public academic and professional showcase. All published articles must adhere to journalism ethics, respect intellectual property, and feature original reporting. Plagiarism and unethical conduct will lead directly to course failure.
{: .lesson-warning}

#### Workflow: Individual Sites and Group Portals

Throughout this semester, you will work across two distinct digital environments. **Weekly Exercises** (worth 5 points each) are executed on your personal **Individual Testing Site**. You will then apply all acquired skills collaboratively to your team's live production **Group Portal**.

| Week | Topic | Practical Lab | Task & Details |
| :---: | :--- | :---: | :--- |

| **2** | Domain Registration, Web Hosting & FTP Setup | **Practical 1** | Server provisioning, DNS configuration, and verified FTP connection testing (Individual Site). |
| **3** | WordPress Installation & User Role Management | **Practical 2** | WP core installation, database configuration, and editorial user role assignments (Individual & Group Sites). |

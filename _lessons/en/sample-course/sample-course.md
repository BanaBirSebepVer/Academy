## 1. Course Description & Scope
This course provides a comprehensive introduction to modern computational methods at the intersection of social science and computer science. Students will gain hands-on experience in automated data collection (web scraping), natural language processing (NLP), social network analysis, and reproducible research design.

## 2. Weekly Syllabus

### Week 1: Introduction to Computational Methods
- Digital transformation in social sciences and big data
- Open science principles and reproducible workflows
- Python environment setup and scientific computing stack

### Week 2: Automated Data Extraction & APIs
- Fundamentals of web scraping
- Querying structured data via REST APIs
- Legal and ethical considerations in data harvesting

### Week 3: Text as Data & Sentiment Analysis
- Preprocessing pipelines in natural language processing (NLP)
- Tokenization, tf-idf, and topic modeling
- Dictionary-based and machine-learning sentiment classification

### Week 4: Social Network Analysis (SNA)
- Network theory and graph metrics (centrality, density, clustering)
- Network visualization using NetworkX and Gephi
- Community detection algorithms and information diffusion

## 3. Recommended Readings
- Lazer, D., et al. (2020). *Computational Social Science: Obstacles and Opportunities*. Science.
- Salganik, M. J. (2018). *Bit by Bit: Social Research in the Digital Age*. Princeton University Press.

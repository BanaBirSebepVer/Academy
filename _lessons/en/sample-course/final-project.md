This is a collaborative group capstone, though each student receives an individual grade. Each member must author and publish at least **10 original articles** and submit documentation.

**Evaluation Criteria:**

- Group Domain & Server Setup
- CMS Hardening & Editorial Role Assignment
- Theme Customization & Mobile Responsiveness
- Analytics & Webmaster Tools Integration
- Static Architecture & Menu Navigation
- Individual Contribution Report
- Published Articles & Reporting:
  - Editorial depth & writing quality
  - Adherence to journalism ethics
  - Originality & proper sourcing
  - Keyword research & on-page SEO
  - Visual layout & readability

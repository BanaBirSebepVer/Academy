### Abstract
This research explores the convergence between network society theories and computational social science methodologies in the digital age. By integrating large-scale data analytics, social network modeling, and public discourse dynamics, the paper demonstrates how empirical inquiries can be constructed under open science standards.

### Key Contributions
- Empirical evidence on network centrality metrics and information diffusion velocities.
- Benchmark evaluations of language models in large-scale social text classification.
- Proposed standards for algorithmic transparency and research reproducibility.

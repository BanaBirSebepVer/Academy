Students who successfully complete this course will be able to:

- Understand the fundamental dynamics of digital journalism and modern web technologies.
- Manage domain names, web hosting environments, DNS records, and secure FTP deployments.
- Install, configure, secure, and administer the WordPress content management system.
- Implement search engine optimization (SEO) techniques and digital analytics tools.
- Collaborate effectively in teams to design, launch, and maintain a professional online news portal.
